Our Story

How NexMart grew from a dorm-room idea to Ghana's leading e-commerce platform.

Our Mission

Making Quality Shopping Accessible to Everyone

NexMart was founded in 2020 by a team of University of Ghana students who believed that world-class online shopping shouldn't be limited to people in major western cities. We started with just 50 products and three team members operating from a single room on campus.

Today, NexMart carries over 14,700 products across 40+ categories, serves customers in all 16 regions of Ghana, and processes over 10,000 orders every single day. Our mission is simple: bring every product you could ever want to your doorstep, fast, safe, and affordably.
